Build a real-time, painterly-realistic scene of the oasis of Madinah (Yathrib) in the time of the Prophet ﷺ,
about 622-632 CE, at golden hour, that runs in the browser. It only needs to look beautiful and let me walk
(and, with a key, fly) through it. It is an artistic reconstruction, not an exact one.

PRIORITIES (in this order when they conflict)
1. Visual quality
2. Stable frame pacing at the target spec
3. Small bundle size

CONSTRAINTS
- No Three.js/Babylon.js or other engines. WebGPU rendered.
- Everything procedural: no texture/model/audio files. Deliverable is a single self-contained index.html.
  Report minified size. Zero per-frame JS allocations; preallocate all buffers.
- Sacred and historical limits: no human figures at all; no depiction of the Prophet ﷺ, his family, the
  Companions or any person; no animals that would suggest people (a few birds are fine). No interiors of the
  Prophet's ﷺ rooms, no graves. No synthesized adhan, no Qur'an recitation, no scripture text in the world.
  Nothing modern anywhere in view. No minarets, domes or later symbols on the first mosque.
- Geography is composed and compressed, not to scale; every uncertain detail is an informed artistic guess
  and is marked as such in NOTES.md.

TARGET
- Omarchy Linux, Chrome 157, RTX 3070, 60 Hz+ monitor
- 2560x1440 at full resolution. GPU frame time <= 11 ms average, p99 <= 16 ms during a scripted 60 s
  fly-through.

SCENE
- The first mosque: a square enclosure of mud-brick walls on a stone footing, palm-trunk columns carrying a
  roof of palm stalks, fronds and mud over the qibla (south) side, an open earth-and-pebble courtyard, the
  shaded Suffah at the back (north), gates on the north, east and west. The rooms of the Prophet's ﷺ
  household adjoin its east side, shown only as plain closed exteriors.
- The oasis: dense date-palm groves in mud-brick garden walls, wells with wooden frames, earthen irrigation
  ditches, scattered settlements of flat-roofed mud-brick houses with palm-trunk beam ends, fortified stone
  tower-houses (utum), lanes of packed earth
- Landforms: the black basalt of the harrat to the east and south (and west, far), the sandy bed of Wadi
  al-Aqiq with acacias, a low rocky hill, Jabal Sal' close to the north-west, Mount Uhud as a long red granite
  massif on the northern horizon, Jabal Ayr low and dark to the south
- Light: low western sun, long shadows, warm haze turning grey-blue with distance, thin high cloud, sun shafts
  through palm fronds and dust
- Life: a few doves circling, small birds heard in the groves, dust motes drifting in the light
- A small, simple early mosque at Quba to the south, among palms
- World: bounded, but the boundaries can't be visible from the starting area

CONTROLS
WASD walk, mouse look (pointer lock), Shift to run, F toggles flight (then Q/E down/up), 1-6 viewpoints

VERIFICATION
- Visual critic: a separate subagent that sees ONLY the renders, never the code or reasoning. Six fixed camera
  poses at 2560x1440 in GPU-accelerated Chrome, plus frames from the scripted fly-through. It returns
  prioritized, concrete defects. Iterate until it reports nothing above "minor" (at most 6 critic rounds).
- Performance: GPU timestamp queries plus a frame-time log from the scripted fly-through. Report average,
  99th percentile and max.

DONE WHEN: both verification gates pass (or 6 critic rounds are spent). Deliver index.html, the bundle size,
the perf report and the critic's final review.
