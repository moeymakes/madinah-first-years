# Critic, iteration 15 (sonnet subagent, images only) - round 6 of 6, final

1. MAJOR: the mosque doesn't read as the heart of the place (pose-6, flight frames 16-18). From the air a big flat pale-grey rectangle inside low walls; floor smooth and plaster-like, not earth; the shaded palm-frond prayer hall and the east rooms can't be picked out; looks like an empty plaza. Should be: a dark-roofed thatch hall along one side, a visibly earthen courtyard, the lean-to rooms on the east wall, a clear lane leading to it.
2. MAJOR: the lava field reads as brown mud, not black basalt (pose-4; flight 13-18; pose-6 right). In pose-4 it fills about 60% of the frame as a low-contrast chocolate surface with smooth blobby boulders; in pose-6 and the flight frames it ends in a crisp rounded scalloped edge with a bright rim line, like a decal. Should be near-black angular vesicular rock, varied blocks, a ragged blended boundary, sand drifting in.
3. MAJOR: the wadi bed is noisy and white (pose-6 left; flight 7-9, 12): grey-white glittering speckle like a frozen river, a different colour family from the warm sand, will shimmer.
